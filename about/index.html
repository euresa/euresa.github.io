About Me

Hey, I'm Sam! I'm a Senior Software Engineer at NVIDIA, working on systems design and infrastructure. I studied Mathematics at Duke, where I got hooked on using math and code to tackle real-world problems — including a stint on the Quantifying Gerrymandering Project, whose work made it all the way to the NC Supreme Court.

These days I split my time between San Francisco, London, and the Outer Banks of NC — where I grew up. I'm happily married. When I'm not at a keyboard you'll find me at the climbing gym, out for a run, or in the ocean surfing or spearfishing.
