About Me

I'm Sam Eure — a Senior Software Engineer at NVIDIA, where I've worked since late 2023. My work sits at the intersection of systems design, Python, and large-scale software infrastructure. Before NVIDIA I was a software engineer at SchooLinks and Paratus, where I built backend systems with Django and Python.

I hold a B.S. in Mathematics from Duke University. As an undergraduate I was part of the Quantifying Gerrymandering Project, where I helped develop a C++ codebase and statistical simulations for sampling redistricting plans — work that was submitted as evidence in NC Supreme Court cases. I also completed a graduate-level independent study in deep learning, researching and implementing modern neural network architectures using TensorFlow.

Earlier in my career I worked as a Marketing Data Scientist at Valassis Digital, applying Python and statistical modeling to large marketing datasets.

Since 2021 I've been splitting my time between San Francisco, London, and the Outer Banks of North Carolina — where I grew up. I'm happily married. Outside of work you'll find me at the climbing gym, out on a run, in the ocean surfing, or underwater spearfishing.
